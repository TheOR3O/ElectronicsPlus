
Electronics+
=======
Adding electrical components to your world.
> [!WARNING]
> **This mod is still in beta development, so bugs are to expect!**

What does it add?
-------
Electronics+ adds many electrical components, such as:
* LEDs
* Resistors
* Microcontrollers
* Wires
* Fans
* Batteries (Rechargeable/Non-Rechargeable)
* Lights
* Buzzers
* And much more!
